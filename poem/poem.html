Notes From a Tab I Forgot to Close

My laptop fan starts talking before I do.
It is 1:17, and there are three tabs open: Canvas, a recipe I will not make, and a blue word that goes somewhere else. I click it anyway.

Things still on my screen:

  • one password that is almost right
  • an unfinished sentence with no period
  • a weather tab insisting it will rain at 4:00

Things I meant to do before Sunday:

  1. answer the email
  2. put away the laundry
  3. drink the water next to the keyboard

The cursor keeps blinking after I stop typing. It does not look patient. It just keeps making a tiny empty room, then taking it back.
