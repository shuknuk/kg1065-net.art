A Small Index of Departures

At 8:03, I close one tab and open the window.
The room keeps its quiet; the browser keeps its blue light. I leave a small doorway for the next version of me.

Things I carry:

  • a password I almost remember
  • an unfinished sentence
  • the sound of rain behind a loading screen

Things I leave behind:

  1. the first draft
  2. the second guess
  3. one empty chair at the table

Somewhere, a page refreshes without asking. I do not disappear; I become a link, a pause, a bright cursor waiting for the next word.
